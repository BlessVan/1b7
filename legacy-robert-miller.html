His Story

Robert was born and raised in a small farming town, where he learned early on the value of hard work and a good sense of humor. He served in the Navy in his twenties before settling down and raising three children with his wife of 48 years.

Fishing at dawn was his quiet ritual — he rarely missed a sunrise on the lake, rain or shine. He was known around town for his jokes, his patience with anyone who needed help fixing something, and the garden he tended every summer, which fed half the neighborhood in tomatoes alone.

Robert's table was always open. Neighbors, grandchildren, and old Navy friends all found a seat there over the years. He is remembered as steady, funny, and endlessly kind.
