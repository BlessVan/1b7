Scams / Fraud

Report to the FTC

Common scams to watch for:

  • Phishing emails/texts posing as your bank or a familiar company
  • Romance scams asking for money from someone you met online
  • Tech support pop-ups or calls asking for remote access or payment
  • Fake prize or lottery messages asking for a fee
  • Callers posing as the IRS, Social Security, or other government agencies
